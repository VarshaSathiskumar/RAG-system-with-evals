# Publication 519 (2025), U.S. Tax Guide for Aliens — 6. Dual-Status Tax Year › When and Where To File

Part 28 of 60 of Publication 519 (2025), U.S. Tax Guide for Aliens (source document irs-pub-519). Section: 6. Dual-Status Tax Year › When and Where To File.

#### When and Where To File

If you are a resident alien on the last day of your tax year and report your income on a calendar-year basis, you must file no later than April 15 of the year following the close of your tax year (but see the *Tip*, later). If you report your income on other than a calendar-year basis, file your return no later than the 15th day of the 4th month following the close of your tax year. In either case, file your return with the address for dual-status aliens shown on the back of the Instructions for Form 1040.

If you are a nonresident alien on the last day of your tax year and you report your income on a calendar-year basis, you must file no later than April 15 of the year following the close of your tax year if you receive wages subject to withholding. If you report your income on other than a calendar-year basis, file your return no later than the 15th day of the 4th month following the close of your tax year. If you did not receive wages subject to withholding and you report your income on a calendar-year basis, you must file no later than June 15 of the year following the close of your tax year. If you report your income on other than a calendar-year basis, file your return no later than the 15th day of the 6th month following the close of your tax year. In any case, mail your return to the address under *What and Where To File for a Dual-Status Year* in the Instructions for Form 1040-NR.

### Tip:

If the regular due date for filing falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the due date is the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday.
